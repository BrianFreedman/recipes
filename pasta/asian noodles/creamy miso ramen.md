# Creamy Miso Ramen with Ginger-Garlic Chicken #

## Ingredients ##

### For the Multi-Layered Dashi ###
* 2 litres (8 cups) cold water
* 2 squares (10x10 cm / 4x4 inch each) kombu (dried kelp)
* 12 to 16 small dried shiitake mushrooms <sup>1</sup>
* 2 loose cups (approx. 20–24 grams) bonito flakes (Katsuobushi)

### For the Creamy Miso Tare ###
* 90 ml (6 tbsp) white miso paste (Shiro Miso)
* 30 ml (2 tbsp) red miso paste (Aka Miso)
* 30 ml (2 tbsp) tahini or white sesame paste (Nerigoma)
* 2 cloves garlic, finely grated <sup>2</sup>
* 10 ml (2 tsp) fresh ginger, finely grated <sup>3</sup>
* 30 ml (2 tbsp) mirin
* 10 ml (2 tsp) toasted sesame oil

### For the Ginger-Garlic Chicken Breast ###
* 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
* 5 ml (1 tsp) garlic powder
* 5 ml (1 tsp) onion powder
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) salt
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) black pepper
* 30 ml (2 tbsp) soy sauce
* 30 ml (2 tbsp) mirin
* 5 ml (1 tsp) fresh ginger, finely grated <sup>4</sup>
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) neutral cooking oil (divided)

### For the Vegetables & Toppings ###
* 4 large eggs (straight from the fridge)
* 250 ml (1 cup) sweet corn (canned or frozen)
* 10 ml (2 tsp) butter
* 2 large handfuls fresh baby spinach (left raw)
* 2 small carrots, cut into thin matchsticks
* 4 scallions (green onions), sliced paper-thin
* 4 portions fresh or instant ramen noodles

### Substitutions ###
* <sup>1</sup> 20 to 28 mini dried shiitake mushrooms
* <sup>2</sup> 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) garlic powder
* <sup>3</sup> 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) ground ginger
* <sup>4</sup> 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) ground ginger

## Instructions ##
1. Place 2 litres (8 cups) of cold water, 2 kombu squares, and 12 to 16 small dried shiitake mushrooms into a large pot. Let sit on the counter for 1 to 2 hours (or in the fridge overnight) to cold-brew the dashi base
1. Bring a medium pot of water to a rolling boil and gently lower the 4 cold eggs into the water. Boil for exactly 6 minutes and 30 seconds, then immediately transfer them to a bowl of ice water for 10 minutes before peeling carefully and setting aside
1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the white miso, red miso, tahini, grated garlic (or garlic powder substitution), grated ginger (or ground ginger substitution), mirin, and toasted sesame oil until completely smooth to create the tare. Set aside
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Pat the 4 chicken breasts dry and rub both sides evenly with the garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper, then place them in a lightly oiled baking dish and bake for 15 to 18 minutes until the thickest part reaches 160°F (71°C)
1. Place the large dashi pot on the stove over low heat while the chicken bakes, bringing it slowly to a bare simmer. Just before it boils, fish out and discard the kombu, turn off the heat completely, drop in the bonito flakes, and let steep with the mushrooms for exactly 5 minutes
1. Pour the broth through a fine-mesh strainer lined with a paper towel into a clean pot without squeezing the bonito flakes, and keep the broth warm on the lowest stove setting
1. Remove the hydrated shiitake mushrooms from the strainer, discard the woody stems, and thinly slice the caps. Heat 10 ml (2 tsp) of neutral cooking oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat and sauté the mushroom slices and carrot matchsticks together for 2 to 3 minutes until slightly softened but crisp, then set aside
1. Melt 10 ml (2 tsp) of butter in the same skillet over medium-high heat, add the sweet corn, and cook without moving for 1 to 2 minutes until slightly charred before removing and setting aside
1. Mix 30 ml (2 tbsp) soy sauce, 30 ml (2 tbsp) mirin, and 5 ml (1 tsp) grated ginger (or ground ginger substitution) in a small cup. Heat the skillet back up over medium-high heat with 5 ml (1 tsp) of neutral cooking oil, sear the baked chicken for 1 minute per side, pour in the glaze to bubble for 30 seconds, then let the chicken rest on a board for 5 minutes before slicing thin
1. Bring a separate large pot of water to a boil and cook your 4 portions of ramen noodles according to package instructions, then drain thoroughly
1. Turn the heat completely off under the warm dashi pot, ladle a splash of the hot broth into your bowl of mixed miso tare, whisk until smooth, and stir that mixture back into the main pot of dashi until the soup is opaque and creamy
1. Divide the raw baby spinach evenly into the bottom of four large ramen bowls, ladle the hot miso-dashi broth directly over top to wilt it instantly, and divide the drained noodles between the bowls
1. Fan out the sliced chicken breast on one side of each bowl, slice the ramen eggs in half lengthwise to place face-up in the centers, arrange neat piles of charred corn, sautéed carrots, and shiitake mushrooms around the sides, and garnish generously with the raw sliced scallions to serve steaming hot
