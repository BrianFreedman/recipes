# Irish Stew #

## Ingredients ##
* 0.454 kg (1 lb) ground beef
* 1 onion, diced
* 2 carrots, peeled and sliced
* 4 celery stalks, sliced
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 75 ml (5 tbsp) gravy mix
* (1.5 lb) baby potatoes, quartered
* 960 ml (1 cup) beef broth
* 5 ml (1 tsp) light soy sauce
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) dark soy sauce
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) dried parsley
* 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) dried thyme
* 3 bay leaves
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) salt
* 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) monosodium glutamate [*optional*]
* oil


## Instructions ##
1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. When hot, add 10 ml (2 tsp) oil, and then the beef. Cook, breaking up beef into smaller pieces, until no pink remains. Drain and discard any excess grease and set aside.
1. Reduce heat to medium, then add 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter, onion, carrots, celery, and garlic to the same pot. Cook until the mirepoix softens. Add the beef and gravy spice blend, and mix to coat.
1. Add broth, soy sauce, parsley, thyme, bay leaves, salt, and monosodium glutamate. Bring to a boil, then reduce to low. Simmer until potatoes are soft.
