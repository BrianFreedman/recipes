# Irish Stew #

## Ingredients ##
* 10 ml (2 tsp) oil
* 0.454 kg (1 lb) ground beef
* 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter
* 2 onions, diced
* 0.454 kg (1 lb) baby carrots, chopped
* 6 celery stalks, diced
* 6 cloves garlic, minced
* 0.68 kg (1.5 lb) baby potatoes, quartered
* 960 ml (4 cups) beef broth
* 240 ml (1 cup) Guinness beer
* 5 ml (1 tsp) light soy sauce
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) dark soy sauce
* 5 ml (1 tsp) Worcestershire sauce
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) dried parsley
* 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) dried thyme
* 3 bay leaves
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) salt
* 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) monosodium glutamate [*optional*]
* 120 ml (1/2 cup) gravy spice blend


## Instructions ##
1. Heat a large pot over medium-high heat. When hot, add the oil and ground beef. Cook, breaking up the beef into smaller pieces, until no pink remains. Drain and discard any excess grease, then set the beef aside.
1. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the butter, onion, carrots, celery, and garlic to the same pot. Cook until the vegetables soften. Return the cooked beef to the pot and stir to combine.
1. Add the potatoes, beef broth, Guinness beer, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, parsley, thyme, bay leaves, salt, and monosodium glutamate. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to low. Cover and simmer for 15 to 20 minutes, or until the potatoes are fork-tender.
1. Whisk the gravy spice blend with a few tablespoons of water or extra broth until smooth, then stir it into the pot. Simmer for another 2 to 3 minutes, stirring constantly, until the soup thickens into a rich stew.
