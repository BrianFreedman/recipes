# French Beef Stew #

## Ingredients ##
* 4 slices thick-cut bacon, sliced into 6 mm (1/4 inch) strips
* 45 ml (3 tbsp) all-purpose flour
* Salt and freshly cracked black pepper, to taste
* 2 large yellow onions, diced
* 6 large carrots, peeled and cut into 2.5 cm (1 inch) rounds
* 4 celery stalks, cut into 2.5 cm (1 inch) pieces
* 4 cloves garlic, minced
* 37.5 ml (2.5 tbsp) tomato paste
* 5 ml (1 tsp) dried thyme
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) dried rosemary, crushed
* 240 ml (1 cup) dry red wine
* 480 ml (2 cups) high-quality beef broth
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) garlic powder
* 2 dried bay leaves
* 1.36 kg (3 lb) Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cut into 3.8 cm (1.5 inch) chunks

## Instructions ##
1. Place a large, deep stockpot over medium heat. Fry the bacon strips until golden brown and crispy. Use a slotted spoon to transfer the bacon to a side plate, leaving the rendered fat in the stockpot.
1. Increase the heat to medium-high. Working in three separate small batches to avoid crowding the pot, place the beef chunks in the hot bacon fat. Sear undisturbed for 2 to 3 minutes per side until a deep, dark brown crust forms. Transfer the seared beef to the plate with the bacon.
1. Sprinkle the flour, a generous pinch of salt, and cracked black pepper over the seared beef and bacon. Toss gently until the meat is evenly coated.
1. Reduce the heat to medium. Add half of the diced onions, carrots, and celery to the stockpot. Sauté for 4 minutes until they begin to soften, then add the remaining half of the vegetables. Sauté for an additional 4 minutes so all veggies caramelize evenly in the remaining fat.
1. Stir the minced garlic and tomato paste into the vegetables. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring constantly, until the paste darkens and evenly coats the vegetables.
1. Add the dried thyme and dried rosemary directly to the hot vegetables and fat. Stir continuously for 60 seconds until the dried herbs bloom and become highly fragrant.
1. Pour in the dry red wine. Bring to a boil, using a long wooden spoon to vigorously scrape up all the dark, caramelized bits (fond) stuck to the bottom of the stockpot. Simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until the liquid reduces by half and the harsh alcohol aroma cooks off.
1. Pour in the beef broth. Stir in the garlic powder, add the dried bay leaves, and add a generous pinch of salt to balance the natural sweetness of the carrots. Return the seared beef, bacon, and any resting juices to the stockpot and stir well.
1. Bring the liquid to a boil, then immediately turn the heat down to the lowest setting. Cover the stockpot tightly with a lid and simmer gently for 1.5 hours, checking occasionally to ensure it maintains a very gentle bubble.
1. Open the lid and stir in the large Yukon Gold potato chunks, gently submerging them into the gravy and meat. Cover the stockpot again and continue to simmer for an additional 45 to 60 minutes.
1. Test the stew; it is complete when a spoon cuts through a potato chunk and a piece of beef with zero resistance. Turn off the heat, discard the bay leaves, and let the stew rest for 5 minutes to thicken into a rich gravy before serving.
