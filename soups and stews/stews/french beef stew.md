# French Beef Stew #

## Ingredients ##
* 4 slices thick-cut bacon, sliced into 6 mm (1/4 inch) strips
* 1.36 kg (3 lb) beef chuck roast, cut into 5 cm (2 inch) chunks <sup>1</sup>
* 340 g (3/4 lb) cremini mushrooms, cleaned and quartered 
* 45 ml (3 tbsp) all-purpose flour
* Salt and freshly cracked black pepper, to taste
* 2 large yellow onions, diced
* 3 to 4 small shallots, chopped
* 6 large carrots, peeled and cut into 2.5 cm (1 inch) rounds
* 4 celery stalks, diced
* 4 cloves garlic, minced
* 37.5 ml (2.5 tbsp) tomato paste
* 5 ml (1 tsp) dried thyme
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) dried rosemary, crushed
* 240 ml (1 cup) dry red wine
* 480 ml (2 cups) high-quality beef broth
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) garlic powder
* 2 dried bay leaves
* 1.36 kg (3 lb) baby potatoes

### Substitutions ###
* <sup>1</sup> 1.36 kg (3 lb) beef stew meat (sirloin or round)

## Instructions ##
1. Place a large, deep stockpot over medium heat. Fry the bacon strips until golden brown and crispy. Use a slotted spoon to transfer the bacon to a side plate, leaving the rendered fat in the stockpot.
1. Increase the heat to medium-high. Working in three separate small batches to avoid crowding the pot, place the beef chunks in the hot bacon fat. Sear undisturbed for 2 to 3 minutes per side until a deep, dark brown crust forms. Transfer the seared beef to the plate with the bacon.
1. Drop the quartered cremini mushrooms directly into the remaining hot fat. Sauté undisturbed for 3 to 4 minutes until they release their liquid, dry out, and take on a deep golden-brown color. Transfer the mushrooms to the plate with the beef and bacon.
1. Sprinkle the flour, a generous pinch of salt, and cracked black pepper over the seared beef, bacon, and mushrooms. Toss gently until everything is evenly coated.
1. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the chopped shallots, diced celery, and half of the diced onions and carrots to the stockpot. Sauté for 4 minutes until they begin to soften and the shallots catch some color, then add the remaining half of the yellow onions and carrots. Sauté for an additional 4 minutes so all veggies caramelize evenly in the remaining fat.
1. Stir the minced garlic and tomato paste into the vegetables. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring constantly, until the paste darkens and evenly coats the vegetables.
1. Add the dried thyme and dried rosemary directly to the hot vegetables and fat. Stir continuously for 60 seconds until the dried herbs bloom and become highly fragrant.
1. Pour in the dry red wine. Bring to a vigorous boil. Use a long wooden spoon to vigorously scrape up all the dark, caramelized bits (fond) stuck to the bottom of the stockpot. Boil for 2 to 3 minutes until the liquid reduces by half, the bubbles become larger and stickier, and the harsh alcohol aroma cooks off. 
1. Pour in the beef broth. Stir in the garlic powder, add the dried bay leaves, and add a generous pinch of salt to balance the natural sweetness of the carrots, onions, and shallots. Return the seared beef, bacon, mushrooms, and any resting juices to the stockpot and stir well.
1. Bring the liquid to a boil, then immediately turn the heat down to the lowest setting. Cover the stockpot tightly with a lid and simmer gently for 1.5 hours, checking occasionally to ensure it maintains a very gentle bubble.
1. Open the lid and stir in the baby potatoes (whole, halved, or quartered depending on size), gently submerging them into the gravy, veggies, and meat. Cover the stockpot again and continue to simmer for an additional 45 to 60 minutes.
1. Test the stew; it is complete when a spoon cuts through a baby potato and a piece of beef with zero resistance. Turn off the heat, discard the bay leaves, and let the stew rest for 5 minutes to thicken into a rich gravy before serving.
