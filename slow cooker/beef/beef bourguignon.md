# Beef Bourguignon #

## Ingredients ##

### For the Skillet ###
* 150 g (5.3 oz) bacon, diced
* 1 kg (2.2 lbs) beef chuck roast, cut into 4 cm (1.5 inch) cubes
* salt and black pepper to taste
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) olive oil (if needed)
* 1 large yellow onion, chopped
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 30 ml (2 tbsp) flour
* 240 ml (1 cup) dry red wine (such as Pinot Noir or Cabernet Sauvignon)

### For the Slow Cooker ###
* 240 ml (1 cup) low-sodium beef broth
* 30 ml (2 tbsp) tomato paste
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) Worcestershire sauce
* 2 large carrots, peeled and sliced into 1.5 cm (1/2 inch) thick rounds
* 150 g (1 cup) frozen pearl onions
* 225 g (8 oz) cremini mushrooms, halved
* 2 fresh bay leaves
* 3 sprigs fresh thyme <sup>1</sup>

### For Garnish ###
* fresh Italian parsley, finely chopped

### Substitutions ###
* <sup>1</sup> 5 ml (1 tsp) dried thyme

## Instructions ##
1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, fry the diced bacon until crisp, about 5–6 minutes. Remove the bacon with a slotted spoon and transfer it directly to the bottom of your slow cooker, leaving the rendered bacon fat in the skillet
1. Season the beef chuck roast cubes generously with salt and black pepper. Working in batches to avoid crowding the pan, sear the beef in the hot bacon fat over high heat until a deep, dark brown crust forms on all sides, about 2–3 minutes per side. Transfer the seared beef into the slow cooker
1. Lower the skillet heat to medium. Add the chopped yellow onion to the remaining fat and sauté for 3–4 minutes until soft. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until highly fragrant
1. Sprinkle the flour over the onion and garlic mixture, stirring constantly for 1 minute to cook out the raw flour taste. Pour in the dry red wine to deglaze the skillet, scraping up all the flavorful browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Let the wine simmer and thicken slightly for 2 minutes, then pour the entire skillet mixture into the slow cooker
1. Add the beef broth, tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce, carrots, frozen pearl onions, cremini mushrooms, bay leaves, and thyme sprigs to the slow cooker. Stir gently to mix the liquids and coat the ingredients
1. Cover and cook on Low for 7 to 8 hours (or on High for 4 to 5 hours) until the beef is melt-in-your-mouth tender and the sauce has deeply concentrated
1. Turn off the heat. Discard the bay leaves and wooden thyme stems. Taste the rich gravy and adjust seasoning with additional salt and pepper if desired
1. Ladle the hot beef bourguignon into wide bowls, garnish generously with finely chopped fresh Italian parsley, and serve warm alongside mashed potatoes or crusty French bread
