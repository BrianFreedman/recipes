# Spicy Chili #

Source: [Spicy Slow-Cooked Chili](http://allrecipes.com/recipe/223390/spicy-slow-cooked-chili/)

## Ingredients ##
* 1 kg (2.2 lbs) ground beef
* 2 medium onions, chopped <sup>1</sup>
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) cooking oil
* 6 cloves garlic, minced <sup>2</sup>
* 45 ml (3 tbsp) chili powder
* 5 ml (1 tsp) ground cumin
* 5 ml (1 tsp) ground coriander
* 5 ml (1 tsp) dried oregano
* 0.625 ml (1/8 tsp) ground cinnamon
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) cayenne pepper
* 5 ml (1 tsp) unsweetened cocoa powder [*optional*]
* 240 ml (1 cup) Guinness stout
* 240 ml (1 cup) beef broth concentrate
* 2x 474 ml (16 oz) cans kidney beans, drained and rinsed
* 1x 186 ml (6.5 oz) can chipotle peppers in adobo sauce<sup>3</sup>
* 2 bell peppers, chopped into bite-sized pieces
* 480 ml (2 cups) corn
* 2x 398 ml (13.5 oz) cans tomato sauce
* 10 ml (2 tsp) Worcestershire sauce
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) brown sugar
* 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) monosodium glutamate [*optional*]
* 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
* 5 ml (1 tsp) ground black pepper
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) apple cider vinegar <sup>4</sup>
* ~5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda [*optional*]

### Substitutions ###
* <sup>1</sup> any of:
    * 30 ml (2 tbsp) onion powder
    * 90 ml (6 tbsp) dried minced onion
* <sup>2</sup> 3.75 ml (3/4 tsp) garlic powder
* <sup>3</sup> all of:
    * 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) chipotle chili powder
    * 3 jalapenos, deseeded and diced
* <sup>4</sup> 15 ml (1 tbsp) white vinegar

## Instructions ##
1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and stir in ground beef. Cook and stir until beef is crumbly, evenly browned, and no longer pink, about 7 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside.
1. Heat the cooking oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the chopped onions and cook for 5 to 6 minutes until they soften and turn translucent.
1. Add the minced garlic, chili powder, cumin, coriander, oregano, cinnamon, cayenne pepper, and optional cocoa powder directly into the hot oil with the onions. Stir constantly for 45 seconds until the spices become highly fragrant and darken slightly. Do not let them burn.
1. Pour the Guinness and the beef broth concentrate directly into the hot skillet. Use a wooden spoon to scrape up all the caramelized spice bits stuck to the bottom of the pan while combining the liquids. Remove from heat.
1. Add the ground beef, drained kidney beans, chipotle peppers and adobo, bell peppers, and corn into the slow cooker.
1. Pour the hot, spiced onion, beer, and concentrate mixture from the skillet over the beef. Add the tomato sauce, Worcestershire sauce, brown sugar, MSG, salt, black pepper, and optional fish sauce. Stir well to combine.
1. Cover and cook on Low for 8 hours (or High for 4 hours).
1. Use a slotted spoon to locate and remove the whole chipotle peppers from the chili and discard them. 
1. Just before serving, stir in the vinegar. Taste the chili; if the tomatoes taste too sharp or the beer tastes too bitter, stir in the baking soda in small pinches until the flavor rounds out.
