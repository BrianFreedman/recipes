# Spicy Chili #

Source: [Spicy Slow-Cooked Chili](http://allrecipes.com/recipe/223390/spicy-slow-cooked-chili/)

## Ingredients ##
* 1 kg (2.2 lbs) ground beef
* 1 medium onion, chopped <sup>1</sup>
* 2x 474 ml (16 oz) cans kidney beans
* 4 cloves garlic, minced <sup>2</sup>
* 45 ml (3 tbsp) chili powder
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) cayenne pepper
* 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
* 5 ml (1 tsp) ground black pepper
* 5 ml (1 tsp) ground cumin
* 5 ml (1 tsp) dried oregano
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) white vinegar
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) brown sugar
* 1x 186 ml (6.5 oz) can chipotle peppers in adobo sauce <sup>3</sup>
* 2 bell pepper, chopped
* 240 ml (1 cup) beef broth concentrate
* 1.5x 680 ml (23 oz) cans tomato sauce
* 240 ml (1 cup) Guinness stout
* 480 ml (2 cup) corn
* ~5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda [*optional*]

### Substitutions ###
* <sup>1</sup> any of:
    * 15 ml (1 tbsp) onion powder
    * 45 ml (3 tbsp) dried minced onion
* <sup>2</sup> 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) garlic powder
* <sup>3</sup> all of:
    * 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) chipotle chili powder
    * 3 jalapenos, deseeded and diced

## Instructions ##
1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and stir in ground beef. Cook and stir until beef is crumbly, evenly browned, and no longer pink, about 7 minutes. Drain and discard any excess grease.
1. Add the onion, beef, beans, garlic, spices, vinegar, sugar, peppers, and beef broth concentrate to a slow cooker. Then add the the tomato sauce, beer, and corn. Cover and cook on Low for 8 hours, or High for 4 hours. Skim off any grease and add a little baking soda to reduce acitity.
