# Chicken Fajitas #

Source: [Slow Cooker Fajitas](https://www.cookingclassy.com/slow-cooker-chicken-fajitas/)

## Ingredients ##

### For the Slow Cooker ###
* 2 onions, sliced
* 3 bell peppers, sliced
* 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts
* 120 ml (1/2 cup) salsa
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) cornstarch
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) cold water

### Fajita Seasoning Blend ###
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) chili powder
* 10 ml (2 tsp) ground cumin
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) brown sugar
* 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) garlic powder
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) smoked paprika
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) chipotle chili powder
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) ground coriander
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) pepper
* 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) monosodium glutamate
* 1 lime, zested

### For Serving ###
* 1 lime, juiced
* flour tortillas
* sour cream
* cheese
* lettuce
* salsa
* guacamole

## Instructions ##
1. Place only the sliced onions in an even layer at the bottom of your slow cooker. Set the sliced bell peppers aside in the refrigerator for later
1. Mix all the spices from the Fajita Seasoning Blend in a small bowl. Toss the chicken breasts in the seasoning until fully coated
1. Place the seasoned chicken breasts on top of the onions, then pour 120 ml (1/2 cup) of salsa over the chicken
1. Cover and cook on Low for 6 to 8 hours or on High for 3 to 4 hours
1. During the last 60 minutes of cooking time, stir the sliced bell peppers into the slow cooker so they soften nicely but keep just a hint of crunch
1. Remove the chicken to a cutting board and shred it using two forks. In a small cup, mix 15 ml (1 tbsp) of cornstarch with 15 ml (1 tbsp) of cold water until smooth, then stir this slurry directly into the liquid in the slow cooker to thicken it
1. Return the shredded meat back into the slow cooker, stir in the lime juice, and let everything sit in the warm juices for 5–10 minutes to absorb the flavor
1. Serve the chicken and vegetable mixture warm inside soft, heated flour tortillas, and top with sour cream, cheese, lettuce, extra salsa, and guacamole
