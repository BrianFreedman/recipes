# Instant Pickles #

## Ingredients ##

### For the Jar ###
* 2-3 small cucumbers
* 1 garlic clove
* 3.75 ml (3/4 tsp) dill seed
* 1.85 ml (3/8 tsp) mustard seed
* 10 black peppercorns

### For the Brine ###
* 10 ml (2 tsp) white sugar
* 7.5 ml (1/2 tbsp) salt
* 60 ml (1/4 cup) vinegar
* 60 ml (1/4 cup) water

## Instructions ##
1. Wash the small cucumbers thoroughly. Cut them lengthwise into long, flat slices, and then cut those slices in half crosswise so they fit properly into your clean glass jar. Pack the pieces tightly into the jar
1. Bring a small pot of water to a rolling boil. Drop the whole garlic clove into the boiling water and blanch it for 30–60 seconds, then immediately transfer it to ice water. This deactivates the natural enzymes responsible for turning garlic blue or green when exposed to acid
1. Cut the blanched garlic clove in half and add both pieces to the jar with the cucumbers
1. Add the dill seed, mustard seed, and black peppercorns directly into the jar over the cucumbers and garlic
1. In a small saucepan over medium-high heat, combine the white sugar, salt, vinegar, and water. Bring the mixture to a boil, stirring occasionally until the sugar and salt are completely dissolved
1. Pour the hot brine directly into the jar, ensuring the cucumber slices are completely submerged under the liquid
1. Allow the jar to cool to room temperature, then seal it tightly with a lid and place it in the refrigerator
1. Let the pickles sit in the refrigerator for at least 24–48 hours to develop their flavor before eating
