# Gaeng Garee Gai #

Source: [Yellow Curry with Chicken and Potato](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/yellow-curry-chicken/)

## Ingredients ##

### For the Curry ###
* 2 cans (400 ml each) full-fat coconut milk (used in stages below)
* 60 ml (4 tbsp) Thai yellow curry paste
* 5 ml (1 tsp) curry powder
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) ginger, grated <sup>1</sup>
* 2 cloves garlic, minced <sup>2</sup>
* 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut into bite-sized pieces
* 2 medium Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cut into 2 cm (3/4 inch) cubes
* 3 shallots, diced
* 120 ml (1/2 cup) low-sodium vegetable or chicken broth
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) tamarind paste/concentrate
* 30 ml (2 tbsp) fish sauce
* 25 ml (5 tsp) brown sugar
* 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) monosodium glutamate (MSG)

### For Serving ###
* cooked jasmine rice

### Substitutions ###
* <sup>1</sup> 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) ground ginger
* <sup>2</sup> 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) garlic powder

## Instructions ##
1. Open both 400 ml cans of coconut milk without shaking them. Spoon out 240 ml (1 cup) of the thick, solid coconut cream from the tops of the cans into a large pot or wok over medium-high heat. Let it reduce until thickened and creamy, then stir in the yellow curry paste and curry powder. Reduce the heat to medium-low and cook the paste for 2–3 minutes, stirring constantly, until highly fragrant and the coconut oil begins to separate away from the paste
1. Add the grated ginger (or ground substitution) and minced garlic (or garlic powder) to the paste mixture and cook for 1 minute until highly fragrant
1. Add the chicken breast pieces, cubed potatoes, and diced shallots directly into the pan, tossing to coat them thoroughly in the curry paste. Sauté for 2 minutes to let the chicken surface sear and allow the spice flavors to penetrate the ingredients
1. Pour the remaining liquid from your first can, plus all the remaining thick coconut solids from your second can into the pot. Add the broth, stir well to dissolve the paste completely, and bring the mixture to a gentle boil
1. Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover the pot with a tight lid, and simmer for 12–15 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the potato cubes are completely fork-tender
1. Uncover the pot and stir in the tamarind paste, fish sauce, brown sugar, and MSG. Let it simmer gently uncovered for 2 more minutes to allow the sauce to thicken slightly and bind all the savory, sweet, and tangy flavors together
1. Turn off the heat. Ladle the rich yellow curry into wide bowls and serve hot alongside individual portions of warm jasmine rice
