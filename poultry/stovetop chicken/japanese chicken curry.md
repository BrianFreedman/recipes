# Japanese Chicken Curry #

Source: [Japanese Chicken Curry](https://www.justonecookbook.com/simple-chicken-curry/)

## Ingredients ##

### For the Curry Base ###
* 30 ml (2 tbsp) vegetable oil
* 2 chicken breasts
* 2 large yellow onions, diced
* 4 cloves garlic, minced <sup>1</sup>
* 3 medium carrots, cut into bite-sized pieces
* 0.68 kg (1.5 lbs) Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into bite-sized pieces
* 900 ml chicken broth
* 1 package (140 g) Kokumaro Hot curry roux

### For the Flavor Boosters ###
* 1/2 Fuji apple, finely grated
* 30 ml (2 tbsp) mirin
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) light soy sauce
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) honey
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) ketchup
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) Worcestershire sauce
* 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) monosodium glutamate (MSG)

### Substitutions ###
* <sup>1</sup> 5 ml (1 tsp) garlic powder

## Instructions ##
1. Heat the 30 ml vegetable oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the 2 whole chicken breasts and sear for about 3 minutes per side just to brown the outsides and lock in moisture. Transfer the whole breasts to a plate and set aside (they will still be raw in the middle)
1. Drop the heat to medium. Add the 2 diced onions to the pot and sauté for 8 minutes until translucent and soft. Stir in the 4 minced garlic cloves (or garlic powder substitution) and cook for exactly 1 more minute until highly fragrant
1. Add the 3 carrots and 0.68 kg Yukon Gold potatoes to the pot. Stir-fry them with the aromatic onion-garlic mixture for 2 minutes to let them absorb the residual flavors and oils
1. Pour in all 900 ml chicken broth. Bring to a rolling boil, use a spoon to skim off any white foam from the surface, then lower the heat to medium-low. Cover with a lid and simmer for 15 minutes until the vegetables are tender when poked with a fork. While the base simmers, slice your seared chicken breasts into bite-sized pieces on a cutting board
1. Slide the diced chicken and any resting juices back into the pot. Stir in the grated Fuji apple, 30 ml mirin, 15 ml light soy sauce, 15 ml honey, 15 ml ketchup, 15 ml Worcestershire sauce, and 1.25 ml MSG. Let everything simmer uncovered for 2 to 3 minutes so the chicken cooks through and the grated apple blends into the sauce
1. Turn off the heat entirely. Break the 140 g package of Kokumaro Hot curry roux into small individual squares and drop them into the pot. Stir gently but continuously until all the cubes have completely melted and the liquid is perfectly smooth without any lumps
1. Turn the heat back on to low. Simmer uncovered for 5 minutes, stirring frequently so the starch does not stick or scorch on the bottom of the pot. Once the sauce transforms into a thick, glossy, velvety glaze, remove from heat
1. Serve piping hot over a fresh bed of warm, steamed short-grain white rice
