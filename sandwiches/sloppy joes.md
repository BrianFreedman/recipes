# Sloppy Joes #

## Ingredients ##

### For the Skillet ###
* 454 g (1 lb) ground beef
* 1 yellow onion, finely diced
* 1 green bell pepper, finely diced
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 180 ml (3/4 cup) barbecue sauce
* 60 ml (1/4 cup) ketchup
* 10 ml (2 tsp) dijon mustard
* 5 ml (1 tsp) apple cider vinegar
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) Worcestershire sauce
* 7.5 ml (1 1/2 tsp) chili powder
* 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) chipotle chili powder
* 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) crushed red pepper flakes
* salt and black pepper to taste

### For Serving ###
* hamburger buns, toasted

## Instructions ##
1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, add the ground beef, finely diced yellow onion, and the whole finely diced green bell pepper. Cook until the beef is browned and the vegetables are softened, breaking the meat apart with a spoon for 6–8 minutes. Drain any excess grease from the pan
1. Reduce the heat to medium, add the minced garlic to the skillet, and cook for 1 minute until fragrant
1. Pour the barbecue sauce, ketchup, dijon mustard, apple cider vinegar, and Worcestershire sauce directly into the skillet. Stir in the chili powder, chipotle chili powder, and crushed red pepper flakes to build a smoky heat and balance the extra green pepper
1. Season with salt and black pepper to taste, then lower the heat to medium-low and let the mixture simmer for 5–10 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the beef evenly
1. While the meat simmers, split the hamburger buns and toast them in a dry skillet, under a broiler, or in a toaster oven until golden brown to prevent them from getting soggy
1. Spoon the smoky, warm sloppy joe mixture onto the toasted hamburger buns and serve immediately
