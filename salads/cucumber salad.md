# Cucumber Salad #

## Ingredients ##

### For the Salad ###
* 6 mini cucumbers
* 10 ml (2 tsp) salt
* 2 tbsp green onion, finely sliced

### For the Dressing ###
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) regular soy sauce
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) rice vinegar (or white vinegar)
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) Chinese chili oil
* 15 ml (1 tbsp) white granulated sugar
* 5 ml (1 tsp) sesame oil
* 5 ml (1 tsp) sesame seeds

## Instructions ##
1. Wash the mini cucumbers thoroughly and cut off both ends.
1. Cut the cucumbers into your preferred shape. For a traditional look, place a cucumber between two chopsticks and make thin diagonal cuts along the top. Flip it over and repeat on the other side, then slice it crosswise into bite-sized halves. Alternatively, you can simply slice them into thin rounds.
1. Place the cucumber pieces into a large bowl and sprinkle the 10 ml (2 tsp) of salt over them. Gently massage the salt into the cucumbers and let them sit for 5 to 10 minutes to draw out excess moisture, ensuring they stay maximum-crunchy.
1. Transfer the cucumbers to a colander and rinse them thoroughly with cold water 3 to 4 times to completely remove the salt, then strain well.
1. In a separate medium bowl, whisk together the regular soy sauce, minced garlic, rice vinegar, Chinese chili oil, white granulated sugar, sesame oil, and sesame seeds until the sugar is fully dissolved.
1. Add the rinsed and drained cucumbers to the dressing bowl along with the finely sliced green onions. 
1. Toss everything together gently until the cucumbers are completely and evenly coated in the spicy, tangy sauce. Serve immediately for the best crunch, or chill in the fridge for 15 minutes before serving.
